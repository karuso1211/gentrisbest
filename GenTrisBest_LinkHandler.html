About Us

Who we are

The "One Town, One Product" initiative of the Department of Agriculture features the General Trias Dairy Raisers as its flagship project. While promoting the significance of agricultural development and adaptability, the organization asserts that farmers must be compensated fairly for their continuous efforts and, in turn, attain a better standard of living. In addition, they are the first cooperative of renown in the province of Cavite to manufacture goods from genuine buffalo milk.

Vision

Aming Pananaw

Isang maunlad, malakas, at nagkakaisang kooperatiba na tutulong upang mapaunlad ang kalagayang pangkabuhayan ng mga kasapi at ng buong komunidad sa pangkalahatan.

English Translation

A prosperous, strong, and united cooperative that will help improve the lives of the members and the entire community.

Mission

Aming Misyon

Ang pinaninindigang kontribusyon ng Gen. Trias Dairy RMPC upang makamit ang pananaw na ito ay ang patuloy na pagpapalakas ng kasapian, pagpapaunlad ng kanilang kaalaman at kasanayan, pagsuporta sa pagtaas ng kita ng mga kasapi at pagbibigay ng serbisyo sa anyo ng puhunan, pagbebenta ng mga produkto, at pagproseso ng kanilang produkto.

English Translation

The affirmed contribution of Gen. Trias Dairy RMPC to achieve this vision is to continue strengthening the membership, developing their knowledge and skills, supporting the increase in income of the members and providing services in the form of investment, selling products, and processing their product.
