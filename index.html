Our Story

Local Roots,
Fresh Products

GenTri's Best — the Dairy Box of General Trias — was born from a passion for bringing high-quality, locally sourced dairy products to our community. Nestled in Barangay Santiago, General Trias, Cavite, we believe everyone deserves access to fresh, nutritious dairy without compromise.

From a neighborhood dairy stall to a full online ordering platform, we've grown while staying true to our values: freshness, fair pricing, and genuine community service.

We don't just sell dairy — we bring farm-fresh quality straight to your table.
